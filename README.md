## What BayTips Provides

BayTips is a mobile web training tool built around the day-to-day needs of automotive service advisors and lube technicians. It offers service reference material, customer communication exercises, and repeatable skill practice for use during formal training or as a quick aid on the job.

Alongside detailed service content, the app explains service recommendations through cause-and-effect guidance. It also includes sales scripts, objection practice, follow-up resources, and quizzes. The dark interface is designed for mobile screens and keeps the training experience focused on phones and other compact displays.

## Capabilities

- Find current service information through search and category-based filtering.
- Open comprehensive information pages for specific automotive services.
- Understand the cause-and-effect logic supporting service recommendations.
- Rehearse sales scripts at beginner, intermediate, and closer levels.
- Practice customer objection responses through scenarios and focused objection drills.
- Follow a daily checklist for ongoing soft-skills development.
- Use follow-up scripts and upsell frameworks during customer discussions.
- Reinforce service and communication knowledge with practice quizzes in a dark, mobile-oriented interface.

## How to Use BayTips

A training session may follow this sequence:

1. Locate a service with search, then limit the results using category filters.
2. Read the selected service page and its cause-and-effect explanation.
3. Choose the sales-script level appropriate for the conversation or skill being practiced.
4. Complete an objection scenario or use the dedicated objection drills.
5. Work through the daily soft-skills checklist.
6. Study the follow-up scripts and available upsell frameworks.
7. Take practice quizzes to review service details and communication techniques.
